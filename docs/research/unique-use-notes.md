# Unique-use research: notes on the hand-written lists

From the 2026-10-04 research workflow (one researcher and one adversarial verifier per category, then a completeness
critic). The verified items themselves were imported into tools/unique_uses.py. These are the researchers' and
verifiers' remarks about curated.py; review before acting, as some repeat or are already addressed.

- kalphite group mis-ranked: 'Keris partisan of amascut' is ranked above plain 'Keris partisan', but outside ToA it has the plain partisan's stats (+58 stab/+45 str) and only +15% kalphite damage instead of +33%. Order should be breaching > partisan = sun = corruption > amascut > keris. Sun and corruption only gain extra effects inside ToA; outside they are equal to the plain partisan.
- vampyre group is missing the July 2026 quest rewards from The Blood Moon Rises: 'Sunspear' (+50% damage, +25% accuracy, hits all tiers) should be rank 1 and 'Hallowed flail' (+25%/+25%, 4-tick) rank 2, above Blisterwood flail. This matches Vyrewatch Sentinel/Strategies. Also add the ring "Efaritay's aid", which that page lists as ring1 over the Ultor ring.
- EXCLUDE_MELEE_WEAPON should include 'Bone mace'. It cannot attack anything except rats ('You can only use this weapon against rats.'), but its stats (+60 crush, +55 str, 4-tick) can make the damage model pick it as the best crush weapon.
- EXCLUDE_MELEE_WEAPON only excludes Vesta's/Statius's. It should also exclude the PvP-only (bh) imbues that players can own in the main game: 'Abyssal dagger (bh)', 'Barrelchest anchor (bh)', 'Dragon longsword (bh)', 'Dragon mace (bh)'. The wiki says these can only be used in Daimon's Crater, Castle Wars, Clan Wars, Soul Wars, Fight Pits and POH. Without the exclusion the model may pick them for PvM because they have higher stats (e.g. re:\(bh\)$).
- Missing melee niche groups: corpbane (Corp halves all non-corpbane melee), flying (since 25 Jun 2025 halberds are the only melee that can hit Dawn, Kree'arra and aviansies), golembane (Granite hammer is weapon1 at the 2026 Mad Angel boss; Barronite mace), ratbane (Bone mace at Scurrius), multi-target (Hallowfell, Dragon 2h sword, Dinh's bulwark), Araxxor (Noxious halberd always max-hits araxytes), shades (Gadderhammer).
- specs list is missing several melee special attacks with real uses: 'Dragon 2h sword' (Powerstab AoE), "Dinh's bulwark" (Shield Bash AoE), 'Arkan blade' (Flames of Ralos 30% spec, recommended at Tormented Demons and Scurrius), 'Sunspear' (Seeking Lunge always deals 70% of max hit; finishing a venator with it gives a venator heart), and 'Emberlight'/'Arclight' (Weaken spec, used at Corp to stop stat regeneration). Emberlight and Arclight only appear in the demonbane row, which shows just the single best owned item.
- Do not add 'Zamorakian hasta' to any Corp/corpbane list. Attribute affinities says it deals reduced damage to the Corporeal Beast even though it is classed as a spear; only the 2h 'Zamorakian spear' is corpbane.
