# BiS Bank Tabs

**Your best gear for every style and skill, already laid out in your bank. Next to it: what's better at something else, what saves your best, and what's worth alching.**

Open the bank and it's done. There's nothing to tag, no setup to build and no calculator to run. Every tab is built from what you own and rebuilds itself as your bank changes.

## Why this one

- **Best in slot from your own bank, always there.** Melee, Ranged, Magic, Movement and every skill each get a tab laid out like the equipment screen. Other plugins either sort everything you own into categories, or make you pick a boss in a side panel.
- **Best at something, not just best overall.** Next to each best item you get:
  - whatever beats it at max hit or at accuracy (a strength amulet beside fury);
  - a one-handed weapon beside a two-handed one;
  - over 600 wiki-checked situational picks (banes, special attack weapons, Corp spears, spider-boss boots and more).

  Hover anything to see why it's there.
- **Made for ironmen.** These switch on by themselves for ironman accounts:
  - the cheaper option that saves your best (combat potions beside super combats, the next ammo down, every food worth eating);
  - gear that needs no charges beside gear that does.
- **An Alchables tab.** It lists what's worth High Alchemy, highest value first. An item only shows when alching beats selling it (or, for ironmen, beats the nature rune). It never suggests gear you use, teleports, charged items or raw materials. Right-click an item and pick *Remove-alch* to keep it off.
- **Keeps up with you.** The Slayer tab follows your task. Unread prayer scrolls, the Horn of Bran and spell fragments get their own "Unlocks to use" row, and new raid gear lands in the right tab.
- **Your arrangement stays.** Drag items wherever you like; only a new best-in-slot item takes its cell back. It writes no item tags.
- **It tells you about clashes.** If a plugin that rearranges the same tabs is on (Auto Bank Sorter, Bank Assistant, Bank Sorter or Sorted Bank), it names that plugin and says what it does, so your tabs don't get fought over.

## Tabs

- **Melee, Ranged, Magic.** Your best item for every slot sits in the shape of the equipment screen. Situational gear goes beside it, lined up with the slot it replaces. A set with pieces for several slots on one row, such as your prayer gear, gets its own small equipment screen next to the main one, so its cape sits where a cape goes. For melee that means a slayer helmet, a salve amulet, Inquisitor's, Void, and so on. Below that are your best weapon for each job:
  - Melee: stab, slash and crush, plus dragonbane, demonbane, kalphite and other slayer weapons.
  - Ranged: dragonbane, demonbane, blowpipe and multi-target weapons.
  - Magic: an ancient spellbook staff and a standard spellbook staff. The magic tab also holds every rune, a row per kind: elemental, utility (law under fire), combat and combination.

  **Trade-offs** stand beside the best item too. If you own something that beats it at max hit or at accuracy and gives up the other, it shows next to it: a strength amulet beside fury, a bellator ring beside the ultor, Saika's shroud beside the ancestral hat. Anything your best item beats at both is left out (fury beats a power amulet at both). When your best weapon is two-handed and you own a shield or defender, your best one-handed weapon shows too, ready to wield with it. Hover any of them to see what it does better and what it gives up.

  Then come your special attack weapons, the right ammunition for the launchers in the tab, and your potions. Divine and regular potions both show, because each suits different trips. Each kind shows its best version: super combat, or the attack, strength and defence potions when you have no super combat; bastion or ranging; battlemage or magic; the magic brews; and every restore (super restore, sanfew serum, prayer potion, prayer regeneration, Menaphite remedy). Last is your best food on a row of its own: six by default (up to 16), ranked by what one bite heals you right now. The heal amounts come live from RuneLite's own item stats, the numbers in the Item Stats tooltips, so anglerfish follow your Hitpoints level and new foods count as soon as RuneLite knows them. Below those, after a blank row, are your **blighted supplies** (blighted food and super restores, plus the blighted spell sacks on the magic tab), which only work in the Wilderness and PvP areas.
- **Movement.** Your lightest gear in the shape of the equipment screen: full graceful if you own every piece (colours can mix, and the Agility cape counts as the cape), otherwise the lightest weight-reducing item for each slot, with the ring of endurance. Then stamina potions, teleport jewellery, diary rewards and other teleport items. It also has every teleport tablet and scroll, and the runes for teleport spells.
- **Slayer task.** While you have a slayer task, the Slayer tab leads with the best setup for it from what you own: your best gear for the style the task suits (the first recommended style you can hurt the monster with, preferring one whose bane weapon you own), your slayer helmet, a salve amulet on undead when it beats the helmet, the bane weapon (dragon hunter lance on dragons, arclight on demons, keris on kalphites...) and whatever the task needs in a slot (mirror shield for basilisks, insulated boots for killerwatts, boots of stone in Mount Karuulm...). Gear it only suggests stands beside it, and a shield the task needs means a one-handed weapon. Your prayer gear for that style is drawn as a second equipment screen beside the setup, as on the combat tabs. Then the task's items (bag of salt, rock hammer, ice cooler, light source...) and its potions and food. Every task from every master is covered, bosses included. Without a task the tab is the general Slayer tab.
- **Skills.** Prayer, Slayer, Agility, Thieving, Herblore, Crafting, Fletching, Hunter, Mining, Smithing, Fishing, Cooking, Firemaking, Woodcutting, Farming, Runecraft, Construction and Sailing. Tabs with special equipment (rogue's outfit, angler, prospector, graceful on Agility...) start with your best item for each slot in the shape of the equipment screen, like the combat tabs, with the skill cape (or max cape) in the cape slot and alternatives beside it: the other angler look, gloves of silence, Varrock armour and so on. A slot the skill has nothing for takes an alternative you own for it (Varrock armour, goldsmith gauntlets...), else your lightest gear (graceful, or boots of lightness and the like), as on the Movement tab; the skill's own pieces always come first, with your graceful piece for that slot beside them to swap in for running. Then come your best tools, then the supplies you own for that skill, grouped and in level order. For example, Herblore gives each herb a row: grimy, clean, its secondary, the unfinished potion, then the potion's doses 4 to 1, each in its own column. At the bottom, after a blank row, each skill tab shows its **finished goods**: what the skill makes, grouped by what it is made from (potions, jewellery, d'hide, bows and arrows, smithed items, cooked food, runes and so on), plus logs, ores, gems, raw fish and hunter catches for the gathering skills.
- **Alchables.** Under Sailing, with the High Level Alchemy icon: what you hold that's worth alching, highest High Alch value first.
  - **Which items:** tradeable items where High Alchemy gives at least 100 gp more than selling, after the nature rune (*Alch profit at least* changes the 100). Ironmen can't sell, so for them it's whatever the alch is worth after the rune. Prices are RuneLite's live ones.
  - **What it leaves out:**
    - Anything another tab uses (gear, supplies, food, potions, cosmetics). Finished goods still count, so your yew longbows and rune platebodies are in.
    - Anything you open for what's inside (bird nests, caskets, impling jars), and teleports (jewellery you rub, tablets, teleport scrolls).
    - Anything kept for its charges, such as rings of dueling and recoil, games and binding necklaces, and slaughter bracelets (the wiki's "Items with charges").
    - What gathering skills bring in (logs, ores, gems, raw fish, empty bird nests): those are materials for other skills.
  - **Spare gear:** gear another tab uses still shows when you hold more than one. You keep one and alch the rest (three dragon daggers: alch two).
  - **Your choices:** right-click an item and pick *Remove-alch* to keep it off. *Bring back removed alchables* puts those back.

Only items you own appear. That means your bank, including placeholders, potion storage, and (optionally) what you are wearing and carrying. An item can appear in several tabs. Tabs with nothing in them are not created.

## How "best" is decided

- **Armour, jewellery, capes and blessings** are ranked by their equipment bonuses for the tab's style. Strength bonus counts most, then accuracy, then prayer, with defence as a tie-breaker. New items rank themselves.
- **Melee weapons** are ranked by a small damage model: attack speed, strength and accuracy against a sturdy target. It credits passives such as the scythe's extra hits and the fang's double accuracy roll. The stab, slash and crush picks only consider weapons built for that style.
- **Ranged and magic weapons, ammunition, situational gear, special attacks, teleports and skilling tools** come from hand-ranked lists, best first. Your highest-ranked owned item wins.
- Player-versus-player gear and Wilderness-only weapons never take a main slot. Wilderness weapons get their own situational spot.

## Settings

- Turn each tab on or off.
- **Trade-offs beside best in slot** (on by default): also show what you own that beats a best-in-slot item at max hit or accuracy, and a one-handed weapon beside a two-handed one.
- **Cheaper alternatives** (Ironmen only by default; or Always, or Never): also show the cheaper options that save your best.
  - A combat potion, super attack and super strength beside your super combats.
  - The next tier down of ranging, magic and energy potions, the magic brews, arrows, darts, javelins and cannonballs.
  - Every food that heals at least half what your best food does, past the *Food items* count (up to two rows).
  - Gear that needs no charges beside gear that does. For example, your best weapon without charges beside the scythe, shadow or a trident; a helm beside the serpentine helm; a dragon axe beside the crystal axe; anything that isn't Barrows beside Barrows gear.

  Hover one to see what it saves.
- **Backups per slot**: also show the next best one to three items for each pick, whatever they are better at.
- **Food items**: how many foods the combat tabs show (1 to 16).
- **Count worn & carried items** (on by default): gear you are wearing without a bank placeholder still gets its slot.
- Potions and food in combat tabs, **blighted supplies** in combat tabs, supplies and finished goods in skill tabs, and a blank row between sections.
- **Tab icons**: skill icons (Attack for melee, Run for movement, the cosmetics tab keeps an item), or your best item for each tab.
- **Remove tabs when turned off**: the plugin's tabs disappear when you turn it off and come back when you turn it on.
- **Bring back replaced tabs**: puts back the tabs you had before the plugin replaced them.

## Notes

- The tabs are normal Bank Tags tabs (the core **Bank Tags** plugin must be on). Their contents are worked out from your bank each time, so the plugin never writes tags onto your items.
- **It asks what to do with your own bank tabs.** The first time it starts, if you already have tabs, you choose:
  - *Replace all my tabs*: the bank holds only the plugin's tabs.
  - *Keep my tabs*: the plugin's tabs go after yours. By default, a tab of yours with the same name as one of the plugin's (your own "Slayer", say) is still replaced (backed up), so there aren't two Slayer tabs. Untick the box to keep those too.
  - *Turn plugin off*.

  Your items keep their tags, so `tag:` searches still work, and any removed tabs are backed up: *Bring back replaced tabs* in the settings puts them back.
- **It warns you about plugins that clash with it.** Four hub plugins rearrange the same tabs:
  - Auto Bank Sorter makes its own skill tabs;
  - Bank Assistant lays out the bank its own way;
  - Bank Sorter and Sorted Bank re-sort the items inside tabs.

  If one of them is on, the first-run dialog names it before anything is replaced. After that, the first time you open the bank each time RuneLite starts, a chat message says which plugin it is and what it does. That way you can turn one off before they fight over your tabs.
- The tab menu options work on the plugin's tabs too:
  - **Rename** keeps the tab working under its new name.
  - **Change icon** keeps your pick. Change the *Tab icons* setting to give icons back to the plugin.
  - **Disable layout** keeps the tab in plain bank order until you choose *Enable layout*.
  - **Delete** switches that tab off. Turn it back on in the settings.
- You can drag items around in a tab, and your arrangement is kept, across restarts too. When the tab changes:
  - A new best-in-slot item takes its gear cell back, whatever you put there. Whatever it pushes out moves to a free spot.
  - Any other new item goes into its usual spot if that is free, otherwise the next free spot.
  - An item the plugin stops showing leaves a gap.
  - *Disable layout* then *Enable layout* starts the tab over from the plugin's own layout.
- **Add anything yourself.** Drag an item onto one of the plugin's tabs and Bank Tags tags it into that tab, alongside the plugin's picks. *Remove-tag* takes it back out (the plugin's own picks don't offer it, since the plugin decides those).
- A brand-new tab shows up the next time you open the bank.

## Data

Item lists come from the [Old School RuneScape Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki) under [CC BY-NC-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/). This includes equipment pages, recipes for skill supplies, and item versions. `tools/generate_data.py` rebuilds `tabs.json` from the wiki, and the curated rankings live in `tools/curated.py`. The plugin makes no network requests.
